Where is a distributor losing revenue, margin and cash?

A sales director's view of revenue, rep visits, discounting and receivables, the kind of app I relied on in seven years of sales leadership at a distributor. Built on generated data with documented rules, modelled in SQL with dbt and checked by automated tests; the same model runs in Qlik Sense and Power BI.

Finding 1Sul is the only region falling.

Revenue in Sul is down 16.5% in Jan–Sep 2026 on the same months of 2025, while the other regions grew 5–10%. Visits in Sul fell from 2,722 to 1,426 after one of its two reps left in January.

Finding 2One rep gives margin away.

REP07 discounts 15.1% against 6.0% for the other reps, so gross margin is 13.6% against 21.8%, with lower revenue per customer than the other Lisbon reps. That is about €0.96M of extra discount since January 2024.

Finding 3225 customers have not been visited for 60+ days.

They bought €5.4M in the last 12 months. Customers order more often after a visit and lapse when visits stop, so this is the call list.

Finding 4Livestock farms hold the overdue cash.

Farms are 17% of billing but 84% of receivables more than 90 days overdue, and pay 29 days after the due date on average, against 3 days for the other segments.

Finding 5Two categories are seasonal.

Vaccines sell 2.0 times as much per month in spring as in winter; parasiticides 2.3 times as much in summer. Pharmaceuticals, pet food, accessories and hygiene are flat.

How the numbers are built

  • Data: synthetic, written by a Python generator with a fixed seed: 1,800 customers in four segments and the four mainland NUTS II regions, 400 SKUs in six categories, 12 reps, about 951,000 order lines, 68,000 visits and 131,000 invoices from January 2024 to September 2026. The patterns found here are rules in the generator, documented in its source, so the page can be checked against them.
  • Model: dbt on DuckDB. Staging views read the CSV files; marts form a star schema (dim_customer, dim_product, dim_rep, dim_date, fct_sales_lines, fct_visits, fct_invoices) plus one or two marts per question, with tests for keys, relationships and accepted values. The same model is built in Qlik Sense (load script and master items) and in Power BI (TMDL semantic model).
  • Definitions: net revenue excludes VAT; discount = 1 − net ÷ list-price sales; gross margin = (net − cost) ÷ net. Revenue change compares the period with the same months a year earlier. Region is the customer's region.
  • Snapshot measures: days since visit, the call list and receivables aging are measured on 30 Sep 2026, so they do not follow the period filter. Aging buckets count days past the due date of invoices still unpaid on that day. The seasonal index uses the complete years 2024 and 2025 only.
